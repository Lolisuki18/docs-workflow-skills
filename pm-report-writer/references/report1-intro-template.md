## II. Project Introduction

### 1. Overview
#### 1.1 Project Information
Template fields: **Project name**, **Project code**, **Group name**, **Software type** (Web, Mobile App, ...). Add start/end dates, supervisor and repository link when known. Source: registration form; `[Cần bổ sung]` for anything not given.

#### 1.2 Project Team
| Full Name | Role | Email | Mobile |
|---|---|---|---|
First rows in the original: Lecturer, Leader, Member. Do not copy the sample names/emails/phones. Collect from the user. Contact data is personal: include only what the user supplies.

### 2. Product Background
Template guidance: summarise the rationale for the new product - the history or situation that led to the recognition that it should be built - and the customer or the people who raised the idea/request. Facts from the registration form/domain research the user provides; no invented statistics. (The template's cafeteria text is a sample, not content.)

### 3. Existing Systems
#### 3.1 System name1
#### 3.2 System name2
Template guidance: systems that might help solve the listed problems, or from which features can be learned. Per system (`3.1 System name1`, `3.2 System name2`, ...): brief description, link, system actors, features, pros, cons. Only systems the user names or that are verifiably public; cite source/URL.

### 4. Business Opportunity
Template guidance: the market opportunity or business problem being solved; the market or environment in which the product will be used; a brief comparison with existing products; problems that cannot be solved without the product; fit with market trends or corporate direction. Tie to the 3.x limitations.

### 5. Software Product Vision
Template guidance: a concise vision statement of the purpose and intent of the product and what the world looks like once it exists; balanced between customers' and the developing organisation's needs; idealistic at most, but grounded in real markets, architecture, strategy, cost and resource limits. Consistent with scope in 6.

### 6. Project Scope & Limitations
#### 6.1 Major Features
Template guidance: a **numbered list** of major features, emphasising those that distinguish the product; specific user and functional requirements trace back to them. Sample ID style `FE-01`, `FE-02`, ... Names match the SRS feature names.
#### 6.2 Limitations & Exclusions
Template guidance: product features or characteristics a stakeholder might expect but that are not planned. Sample ID style `LI-1`, `LI-2`, ... Scope rule from the template: out-of-scope requirements are rejected unless so beneficial that scope is enlarged with budget/schedule/resource changes. Must be consistent with Out of Scope in the registration form / Review 2 forms.
